# KinCheckAPI v0.6.4

v0.6.4 adds an explicit linear structural reference layer. `StructuralModel`
accepts a declared stiffness matrix (and optional mass matrix), supports and
beam properties; `transfer_loads()` records every source-to-DOF mapping and
`solve_static_structure()` returns displacements, reactions, residual norms and
model hashes. `solve_buckling_screening()` provides an Euler-column or
linearized eigenvalue screen. Stress, deflection and structural-margin checks
remain indeterminate when material allowables or response evidence are absent.

This release does not claim automatic BREP meshing, nonlinear contact,
plasticity, fracture, or a full CalculiX adapter. Unsupported geometry and
boundary conditions are reported as structured capability or indeterminate
states.
