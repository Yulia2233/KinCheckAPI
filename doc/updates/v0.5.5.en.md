# KinCheckAPI v0.5.5 prepared update

This update closes the backend-independent C-class verification loop for scalar motion. PathTarget and PoseTrajectory support sampled curve, planar, and pose trajectory checks. check_start_stop_reversal, check_periodic_motion, and check_synchronization report event times, drift, explicit target errors, and the first failing sample through the common diagnostic fields.

ProfileBoundary.ERROR rejects profiles that do not begin at zero and reports an out-of-range evaluation as a structured backend step failure. Failed backend steps retain the samples completed before the failure in MotionSolveError and try_solve_motion.

Periodic targets are sampled into finite position profiles, and synchronization checks require an explicit coordinated target instead of treating final positions as the target. The current backend still supports scalar fixed, revolute, and prismatic joints. 6D joints, Cartesian drivers, continuous-time TOI collision solving, and general multi-solution IK remain explicit capability failures; no pose result is presented as proof that those solvers exist.
