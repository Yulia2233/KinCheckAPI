# KinCheckAPI v0.6.1 update

v0.6.1 adds scalar-tree inverse dynamics on top of the v0.6.0 CADIR physical-property bridge and static equilibrium. `DynamicState` and `DynamicRequest` describe each revolute or prismatic joint position, velocity, and acceleration in SI units; `GravityField` and `WrenchLoad` describe gravity and external loads. `solve_inverse_dynamics()` compiles CADIR BREP mass, COM, and full inertia tensors as explicit MuJoCo inertials and returns required joint effort/torque, power, applied-load records, and backend evidence.

`check_dynamic_load_limits()` compares the required effort with declared per-joint force or torque limits. An exceeded rating remains `failed` and cannot be hidden by a static result. Every failure includes `passed`, `what_happened`, `cause`, `how_to_fix`, object IDs, units, and actual/target evidence.

The capability is limited to scalar revolute/prismatic trees without closures, couplings, or general constraints. It does not claim closed-loop reaction sharing, contact response, impact, structural stress, or deformation. Missing MuJoCo, incomplete state coverage, joint-limit violations, and physical-source failures return structured `capability_failed` or `validation_failed` results.

The E01 loaded-arm example adds `examples/dynamics_loaded_arm/verification/verify_dynamic.py`. It rebuilds the physical model from `product.scadpkg`, MJCF, and mapping, checks gravity plus a 2 rad/s² inertial demand at 30°, and compares the result with the 35 N·m short-term rating.

Regression coverage includes gravity/inertia baselines, external loads, rating failures, incomplete states, CADIR physical binding, and unavailable-backend diagnostics.
