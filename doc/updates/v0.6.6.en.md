# KinCheckAPI v0.6.6

v0.6.6 adds a traceable high-cycle fatigue reference chain. `StressHistory`
keeps signed stress samples and case/region identity; `count_cycles()` uses a
rainflow stack; `MeanStressCorrection` supports declared Goodman or Gerber
corrections; and `evaluate_fatigue()` reports Miner damage, life repeats,
residual half-cycles and material-domain evidence. Operating-envelope
evaluation records evaluated and uncovered cases, while drive-duty summaries
retain signed torque, RMS, peak power and positive/negative mechanical energy.

The result is a declared S-N estimate, not a universal fatigue-life guarantee.
Plasticity, crack growth, corrosion, temperature cycling and non-proportional
multiaxial fatigue remain outside this release.
