# KinCheckAPI v0.6.5

v0.6.5 adds linear modal, frequency-response and transient calculations over
the explicit structural model. `solve_modes()` preserves mass-normalized modes
and rigid-body evidence; `solve_frequency_response()` keeps complex FRFs and
declared damping; `solve_transient_response()` records displacement, velocity
and acceleration histories. `estimate_psd()` and `compute_rms()` enforce
uniform sampling, nonnegative PSDs and explicit units. Resonance and vibration
limit checks distinguish frequency proximity from amplitude limits.
